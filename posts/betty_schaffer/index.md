On December 6, 1902, Betty Schaffer of Vienna filed a patent for a device designed to gather and hold dress fabric in place. Its hinged sets of wire pins secured the folds, while a protective bracket covered their tips and a locking hook kept the device closed. The invention allowed the wearer to gather a dress to the desired height and fasten it securely.

Date of Application: 6 December 1902

Date of Issuance: 10 October 1903
