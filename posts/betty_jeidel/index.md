On August 10, 1916, Betty Jeidel of Berlin filed a patent for a thermal switch used in heat and fire alarms. As temperatures rose, an expanding metal tube separated two electrical contacts, triggering the alarm. Her design kept the contacts close together, allowing even slight cooling to reconnect them and make the device ready for use again.

Date of Application: 5 December 1913

Date of Issuance: 10 August 1916
