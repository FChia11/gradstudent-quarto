On March 30, 1927, Betty Hartmann-Würth of Feldmeilen, Switzerland, filed a patent for a listening device to inspect machinery through its sounds and vibrations. Its metal shaft folded like a carpenter’s ruler, making it adjustable and easy to carry in a pocket. Designed for examining engines, pumps, bearings and water pipes, it offered a practical tool for listening to machines at work.

Date of Application: 30 March 1927

Date of Issuance: 1 February 1928
