On November 25, 1938, the Polish firm Inz. M. Lewandowski i B-cia Kasinowscy was granted a patent for Mariam Lewandowski’s device for converting vibrations into rotary or straight-line motion. The invention used an angled spring to turn a wheel or move a flat surface, with the aim of improving efficiency and reducing noise.

Date of Application: 22 May 1937

Date of Issuance: 1 March 1939
