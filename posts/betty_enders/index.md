On September 2, 1908, Betty Enders, a German inventor, patented a device that turned a music-storage cabinet into a versatile companion for musicians. Its tabletop unfolded into angled music stands, while its side panels slid out to hold additional scores. When the music stopped, everything folded neatly back into a table, with sheet music stored beneath.

Date of Application: 14 April 1908

Date of Issuance: 2 September 1908
