On January 16, 1928, Betty Berger of New York filed a patent for an electric water heater designed to bring hot water to the household sink. Her design linked water flow to heating power, increasing the heat as the valve opened. A red light showed when the heater was working, while a white light signalled that it was off.

Date of Application: 16 January 1928

Date of Issuance: 16 October 1928
