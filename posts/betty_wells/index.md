On October 22, 1908, Betty Wells of Chicago filed a patent for an egg-steamer designed to take the fuss out of cooking eggs. Individual cups sat in a removable frame that allowed steam to circulate around them for even cooking. Once breakfast was ready, the whole frame could be lifted out and placed on the table without disturbing the cups.

Date of Application: 22 October 1908

Date of Issuance: 12 October 1909
