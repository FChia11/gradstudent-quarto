On March 26, 1901, Betty Anderson of Lancaster, Kentucky, patented a spoon-holder to solve a familiar annoyance: utensils slipping into serving dishes and cooking pots. Made from a single piece of metal, her simple clip attached to a vessel’s rim, keeping a spoon within reach while allowing the lid to close. 

Date of Application: 21 September 1900

Date of Issuance: 26 March 1901
