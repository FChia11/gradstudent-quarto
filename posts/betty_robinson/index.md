On September 10, 1929, Betty F. Robinson of New York was granted a patent for a bandeau designed as a simple and attractive head covering. Made from a single piece of fabric slit at both ends to form tying sections, her invention could be wrapped securely around the head, making it suitable for motoring or seaside outings.

Date of Application: 13 April 1929

Date of Issuance: 10 September 1929
