On May 12, 1900, Lucié Caillié of Paris was awarded a patent for a novel bottle or jar stopper that created a secure, airtight seal. Her design allowed users to open and reclose the bottle many times while keeping the stopper attached so it would not get lost.

Date of Application: 11 July 1899

Date of Issuance: 12 May 1900
