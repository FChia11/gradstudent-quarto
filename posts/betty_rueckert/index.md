On 31 December 1900, Betty Rückert developed a device for holding sewing, embroidery and mending work securely in place. Her invention attached to the edge of a table and allowed the fabric to be freely turned while remaining taut. Designed as a versatile alternative to an embroidery hoop, it could also be taken into gardens or used during social visits.

Date of Application: 13 October 1899

Date of Issuance: 31 December 1900
